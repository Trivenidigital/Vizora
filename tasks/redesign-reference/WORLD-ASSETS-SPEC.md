Three standalone prerendered scenes replace the code-built café / hotel /
retail vignettes. Everything else — place labels, the sync thread, buttons,
status counts, hover/focus states, the locations cards — stays live HTML on
top. Until the assets land, the page renders the CSS-3D vignettes in the same
slots, so nothing is blocked.

**These are newly generated scenes in the concept board's visual direction —
never crops of `06-little-worlds.png`.**

## The one rule that matters: generate them as a SET

Three independently generated images will read as a collage. Every image must
share the block below verbatim; only the "scene content" paragraph changes.
If the tool supports it, generate all three in one session / with one style
reference / same seed family, and reject the whole set if one drifts.

### Shared block (paste unchanged into all three prompts)

> Miniature architectural diorama, product-render quality, of a single small
> interior set standing on a round two-tier honed limestone plinth (warm
> cream limestone, soft rounded edge, subtle natural veining). Open-front set:
> no ceiling, no front wall, curved or straight back wall only.
> **Camera:** three-quarter elevated view, camera ~28° above the horizon,
> rotated ~20° to the right of straight-on, long lens (≈85 mm, near-
> orthographic, minimal perspective distortion). The whole plinth is visible
> and centred, with clear empty margin of at least 8% on every side — nothing
> (plants, furniture, signage) touches or crosses the image edge.
> **Lighting:** one soft warm key light from the upper left front, gentle
> fill, soft contact shadows falling to the lower right; small warm practical
> lamps glowing inside the set. No harsh specular, no lens flare, no depth-
> of-field blur, no vignette.
> **Materials:** warm cream limestone, walnut timber, brushed brass, off-white
> plaster, deep forest green (#1f4230) accents, small touches of terracotta
> coral (#d96a4c); lush realistic potted plants (olive, fiddle-leaf, fern).
> **Background:** fully transparent (PNG with alpha). If transparency is not
> supported: a perfectly flat, untextured #f5f1e8 background with NO floor,
> NO horizon line, NO gradient, and the plinth's ground shadow fading out
> well inside the image bounds.
> **Must NOT contain:** any UI, labels, captions, pins, connecting lines,
> arrows, watermark, page copy, browser chrome, people's faces in close-up,
> real brand names or logos.

### Scene content — café (`cafe`)

> A small specialty coffee bar: fluted walnut counter with an espresso machine
> and a glass pastry case, three stools, one round café table with two chairs,
> a chalk A-board on the floor. On the back wall, two portrait digital menu
> screens side by side, clearly lit and the brightest things in the scene: the
> left shows a latte-art cup with the words "Specialty Coffee", the right a
> fresh salad bowl with the words "Fresh Options". Timber slat canopy above.

### Scene content — hotel (`hotel`)

> A boutique hotel reception in a curved limestone alcove with a soft arched
> top and warm cove lighting: fluted brushed-brass reception desk with two
> small table lamps, a rug, one navy armchair, two suitcases, tall plants.
> On the back wall, one large landscape digital screen, clearly lit, showing a
> mountain-lake landscape with the single word "Welcome".

### Scene content — retail (`retail`)

> A small fashion boutique framed by a slim black steel arch-and-grid frame:
> a brass clothes rail with neutral-toned garments on hangers, a low walnut
> display table with folded knitwear and small accessories, plants. On the
> right, one tall freestanding portrait digital totem screen, clearly lit,
> showing a fashion figure in a long cream coat (no recognisable face) with
> the words "Style Moves People".

## Delivery format

| | |
|---|---|
| Files | `cafe.png`, `hotel.png`, `retail.png` (we convert to WebP/AVIF) |
| Size | ≥ 1600 px wide, ideally 2400 px; same pixel dimensions and same aspect ratio (4:3 preferred) for all three |
| Scale | the plinth occupies the same fraction of the frame width (~80%) in all three, so relative scale holds when placed side by side |
| Alpha | transparent preferred; otherwise flat `#f5f1e8` exactly |
| Drop into | `web/public/landing/worlds/` |

## Acceptance checklist (reject the set if any fails)

- [ ] Same camera elevation and rotation across all three (plinth ellipses have the same flattening).
- [ ] Shadows fall the same direction (lower right) in all three.
- [ ] Same limestone tone and plinth design in all three.
- [ ] Whole plinth + every plant/prop inside the frame with margin.
- [ ] Background is truly transparent or truly flat `#f5f1e8` — no floor, no halo.
- [ ] Signage is the brightest element and its words are spelled exactly; no garbled text anywhere.
- [ ] No labels, lines, UI, watermark, logos, or recognisable faces.
- [ ] Provenance recorded: tool + model, date, prompt, licence/usage terms → `web/public/landing/worlds/README.md`.

## How they are integrated

`web/src/components/landing/worldAssets.ts` holds one entry per place
(`src`, intrinsic `width`/`height`, optional `matte: 'ivory'`). An entry left
`null` renders the CSS-3D vignette instead; mixed mode is supported. Images
sit in aspect-ratio-reserved slots via `next/image` (`object-fit: contain`,
never stretched or cropped), hero images `priority`, location-card thumbnails
derived from the same files via `sizes`. Signage content is baked into the
image and is illustrative; the page captions it as example content.
