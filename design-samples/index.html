Before any of this touches the app

These are standalone static files under design-samples/. No app code is modified, and this is deliberately separate from the parked feat/design-explorations branch. Nothing ships until you pick a direction.

The recommendation

A or D for the hero, B's skeleton below it

A is the safer, more scalable hero; D is the one that teaches the product in a single image. Either way, use Sample B's structure below the fold — product UI, proof, features, trial. The hero earns attention; B's page converts it.

The one substitution

Your screen is the hero image

Every template on motionsites leans on gorgeous AI-generated 3D. For signage that's a mistake — the product shot is the proof. Replace the rendered object with your dashboard driving a real screen wherever you can.

The open question

Light or dark?

Vizora is dark-first today and the dashboard stays dark either way. Going light on the marketing site is the single biggest decision here — it reads as more premium and makes content imagery pop, but it is a genuine brand break.
