FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does it take to get a screen live?
About five minutes. Install the player, enter the pairing code shown on the screen, assign a playlist. The device joins your fleet and starts reporting status immediately.
What hardware do I need?
Vizora runs on Android TV devices and on Windows, macOS and Linux through the desktop player. Packaged players configure their own auto-start and keep the display awake, so a screen recovers on its own after a power cut.
How do I know a screen has actually gone dark?
Every player holds a live connection to the platform and heartbeats continuously. Status changes reach your dashboard in real time rather than on a polling delay, and predictive monitoring flags devices trending toward failure before they drop.
Can I update pricing across every location at once?
Yes. Change the layer once — a playlist, a daypart or a live data feed — and every screen in the group re-renders. There is no per-screen republish step.
How does billing work?
Per screen, per month, with a 30-day free trial that needs no credit card. Annual billing saves 20%. Basic covers up to 50 screens and Pro up to 100; beyond that, Enterprise is priced on volume.
Is my content and data secure?
Users and devices authenticate on separate credentials, uploads are validated by magic number rather than file extension, access is role-scoped per organization, and every change is written to a retained audit trail. SSO is available on Enterprise.
