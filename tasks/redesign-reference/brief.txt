## Creative direction

Use **Little Worlds + Studio** as the starting direction recommended by Astra. The user has authorized the redesign without explicitly choosing a numbered concept. Treat this as a strong starting brief with room for your own design judgment, not a requirement to reproduce the concept pixel for pixel.

Create a premium, warm, dimensional product identity:

- Ivory and limestone backgrounds, deep forest green, ink text, restrained brass/coral accents.
- Editorial display type for a few key headlines, highly legible sans-serif type for copy and controls.
- An original 3D scene showing a miniature café, boutique hotel and retail shop, with digital signage visibly serving each space.
- Convincing depth through geometry, lighting, shadows, material texture and spatial composition. A few floating flat rectangles or generic gradient blobs do not satisfy the 3D direction.
- A calm, well-spaced page with strong typography, deliberate visual rhythm and clear hierarchy. Give each section a distinct role.
- Emphasize the relationship between real places, their screens and the content users manage.

Suggested headline territory: “Your world. Perfectly in sync.” You may improve all copy. Explain the product directly: Vizora lets businesses create/manage content, organize playlists, schedule playback, and manage signage screens across locations.

The original generated concept board may be supplied alongside this brief as **06-little-worlds.png**. It is a visual reference, not a production asset or a webpage to paste into the site. The original Studio board, **01-studio.png**, is a secondary reference if available. If references are absent, the detailed direction above is sufficient to proceed.

## Rebuild the content narrative

Devise and implement a coherent new narrative. This proposed sequence is guidance, not a mandated reproduction:

1. **Hero: places brought to life.** Plain-language value proposition, distinct 3D scene, primary signup action, secondary action that leads to a real product explanation.
2. **Explore your setting.** Café, hotel and retail interaction with specific grounded examples: changing menus, welcoming guests, seasonal promotions. Show what happens on a screen.
3. **From content to playback.** A focused Create / Organize / Schedule / Display sequence with an illustrative live HTML product preview. Illustrative states must not masquerade as real customer telemetry.
4. **A workspace that makes sense.** Explain a small number of verified strengths using useful visual examples. Confirm capabilities against the repository; remove unsupported claims.
5. **Start with your screens.** Present the actual onboarding path and verified pricing if pricing remains useful to conversion. Fetch from the existing pricing source rather than hardcoding fictional commercial terms.
6. **Useful questions and a clear final action.** Write concise original FAQ copy from verified behavior. Include functioning footer/legal links.

Avoid fictional testimonials, customer logos/counts, guaranteed results, invented uptime, unsupported AI features, fake reviews or made-up free-trial terms. You have full freedom to replace existing marketing copy, not to invent product capabilities.

## Interaction and implementation quality

Choose the lightest implementation that achieves a compelling dimensional experience. If using Three.js/react-three-fiber or another renderer, justify the dependency through the result. If an existing renderer is available, reuse it appropriately. Real-time 3D, prerendered assets plus restrained motion, or a hybrid are acceptable if the final experience is excellent. Report precisely which approach you implement.

- Keep headings, buttons, navigation, pricing and controls as semantic live HTML.
- Do not make the whole homepage a canvas or flattened screenshot.
- Do not crop a reference board into a fake hero. Build a dedicated scene or source/create standalone appropriate assets.
- Use deliberate motion: gentle scene response, clear selected states, restrained section transitions. Keep native scrolling and avoid forced long scroll sequences.
- Provide static and reduced-motion fallbacks; touch users must not depend on hover.
- Reserve image/scene dimensions to prevent layout shifts. Load below-fold assets lazily. Defer heavy 3D work where appropriate.
- Ensure readable contrast, visible focus, logical headings, keyboard controls, accessible mobile navigation and descriptive alternative text.
- All CTAs must lead somewhere real. No inert demo buttons or placeholder links.
- Keep styles scoped so shared application pages do not regress.
- Use appropriately licensed local/self-hosted assets when possible; record asset provenance.

Do not deploy or merge as part of this assignment. Deliver a local preview and reviewable branch/diff; a draft PR is acceptable when repository workflow supports it.
